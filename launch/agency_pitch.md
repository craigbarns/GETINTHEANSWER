# Agency outreach — the $149/mo plan

Target: local SEO agencies, web design shops and SMMA owners in the US.

Why agencies before local businesses: they are reachable, they buy 25 locations
at a time, they do not churn like a plumber on a $29 subscription, and — the
part that matters — **they perform the fix this product does not**. We measure;
they remediate and bill for it. That makes us a tool in their stack rather than
a diagnosis nobody can act on.

---

## The rule this playbook runs on

**Do not pitch. Scan one of their clients first, and lead with the result.**

Every agency site lists clients or case studies. Pick one, run it through
GetInTheAnswer, and open with what the engines actually said about that client.

An agency reading "Botox by Nurse Amy is named by ChatGPT and invisible on
Gemini and Perplexity" is not reading marketing. It is reading a problem with an
account it is being paid to look after. That is a professional obligation, and
it is why this converts where a feature list does not.

It also costs about $0.35 and four minutes per prospect. At 30 prospects that is
roughly $10 and one afternoon.

---

## Volume, and what it means for tooling

This is **20 to 30 personal emails**, sent one at a time from your own address.
It is correspondence, not a campaign.

That means you do **not** need a separate warmed domain, a cold-email platform,
or the CAN-SPAM apparatus in `scripts/outreach_generator.py`. Those exist for
the 900-prospect local-business list, where volume makes them mandatory. Here,
send from the address you actually read replies on.

Never send agency outreach through a bulk tool. The whole advantage is that the
mail is visibly hand-written about their specific client.

## Finding them

- Google `"local seo agency" <city>`, `"seo agency for contractors"`,
  `"medspa marketing agency"` — the ones ranking are the ones spending on
  marketing, so they buy tools.
- LinkedIn: search "local SEO" or "SMMA" with the Owner/Founder filter.
- Their client list is on the site, usually under Work, Clients or Case Studies.
  That name is what you scan.

Prioritise agencies serving the industries already scanned — plumbers, HVAC,
roofers, dentists, lawyers, med spas, chiropractors, pest control, auto repair,
restaurants, real estate. You can hand them city-level context on day one.

## What to say when they reply

**"How is this different from Semrush's AI toolkit?"**
Be straight: Semrush and Ahrefs track brand mentions at scale for enterprise
brands. This tests the specific local questions a customer asks in a named city,
across four engines, and names the competing businesses. Neither is a substitute
for the other — but at 25 local clients, $149 is a different order of cost.

**"Can you fix it, not just measure it?"**
No, and that is the point — you fix it, and bill for it. The report gives you a
prioritised list of what to fix and a before/after to show the client.

**"Do you have agency case studies?"**
You have none. Say so: "You would be the first agency on this — which is why I
will set your accounts up myself and why the first month is on me if you want
it." Inventing a reference is how you lose the deal you were about to win, and
this file previously claimed several agencies were already using it. They were
not.
